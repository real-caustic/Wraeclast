Wraeclast Architect

Path of Exile league-starter and levelling guides, with a loot filter for each build. Works offline once opened.

Loot filters: download the .filter file, then copy it into Documents/My Games/Path of Exile (PoE 1) or Documents/My Games/Path of Exile 2 (PoE 2) on the PC that runs the game, and pick it in Options → UI. Grades are the guide's own, and none of these builds has been playtested yet.
